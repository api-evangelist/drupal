Choosing a CMS for catalogues, multilingual pages, and integrations means comparing how each platform handles relationships, editorial work, and delivery—not just content types on a slide. Drupal vs Contentful , Sanity, and Storyblok take different paths: integrated application vs managed headless services, core translation and moderation vs SaaS localization, and visual editing vs configurable Drupal workflows. Maciej Lukianski walks through content models, permissions, JSON:API options, AI boundaries, and what growth costs look like on each stack.
