AI crawlers already fetch product pages during live conversations, but citations stay rare. Drupal sites cited by AI need one fact in fields, then the same value on the page, in JSON-LD, in feeds and through JSON:API or MCP tools. Maciej Lukianski walks through what fetchers need, which Drupal modules cover Markdown, llms.txt and MCP Server today, and where configuration still decides whether a bot can quote your catalogue.
