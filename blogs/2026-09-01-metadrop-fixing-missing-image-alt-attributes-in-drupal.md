Missing image alt attributes cost accessibility and SEO points Empty alt attributes are an accessibility and SEO killer , and the cost is measurable. Google Lighthouse flags the error on both of its audits: up to 6 points subtracted from the Accessibility score and 8 points from the SEO score. The impact on real users is worse than the score.
