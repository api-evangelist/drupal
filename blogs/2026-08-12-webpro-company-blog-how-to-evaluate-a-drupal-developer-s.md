When you hire a Drupal developer or change maintenance partner, do not ask only for a portfolio. Ask how they keep the system upgradable, testable and understandable for the next developer. In 2026, it is no longer enough to evaluate a Drupal developer simply by knowing that they have "worked with Drupal".
