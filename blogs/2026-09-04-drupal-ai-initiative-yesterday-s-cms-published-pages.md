Author: Will Huggins Imagine asking an AI assistant: " Show me waterproof jackets under £150, in size M, available for delivery tomorrow ". Today, the assistant might search the web, read several retail websites and summarise what it finds. But what if, instead of reading pages, the agent could ask retailers directly which products matched those requirements?
