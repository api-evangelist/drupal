Drupal 7 has reached its end of life, and the jump to modern Drupal is in practice a rebuild, not an upgrade – new architecture, a contrib audit, a theme rewritten from scratch. There has always been a calmer answer, and it carries a name this community knows well: Backdrop CMS, the community continuation of the Drupal 7 lineage. What was missing was the road.
