Someone told me this week, correctly, that ClaudeBot and GPTBot respect robots.txt, so there is no need to block them by other means, and that the really bad bots disguise as Chrome anyway, so a user-agent is useless; watch behaviour, get a WAF. Every fact in that is right and the conclusion is still wrong, and the gap between the two is the story of why the old ways of guarding a Drupal or Backdrop site have quietly stopped working.
