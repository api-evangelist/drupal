The Drupal project runs one of the most disciplined coordinated-disclosure programs in open source, and it has done so through volunteer effort for more than two decades. Millions of sites, many operated by governments, universities, and enterprises, depend on it. This post is the first in a series stemming from the Drupal AI Security Initiative.
