Introduction Adopting AI can feel overwhelming, so I am sharing a few tales from my experience creating, maintaining, and assisting with contributed Drupal modules. AI can do a decent job building a "greenfield" Drupal module from scratch, so my first tale is about using AI to build and contribute a module . For my next tale, I want to talk about using AI to maintain an existing "brownfield" Drupal module.
