Prices, specifications and proof buried in body copy are hard to compare, filter or reuse across pages, feeds and AI answers. Drupal content modeling stores those facts as fields, connects related records with entity references and leaves prose for explanation. Maciej Lukianski walks through buyer-question audits, product and service field tables, migration stages and coverage reports that turn one edit into every output.
