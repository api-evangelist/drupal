A buyer asks an AI assistant about your product, but the answer sits behind a click or an outdated page. A Drupal AI-readability audit checks whether intended services can reach your content, whether HTML delivers useful answers, and whether facts stay consistent across outputs. Maciej Lukianski walks through five layers beyond classic SEO: access, delivered HTML, buyer questions, JSON-LD alignment, and multilingual consistency, plus what actionable findings should look like.
