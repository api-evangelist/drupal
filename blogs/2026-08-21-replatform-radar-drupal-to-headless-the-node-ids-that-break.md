When you move a Drupal site to a headless CMS, the thing most likely to break silently is not your content. It is the invisible wiring between pieces of it. Drupal stores relationships as numeric IDs (node 4127, term 88, media 903), and almost every headless platform mints brand-new IDs the moment you import.
