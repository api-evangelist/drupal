Take Away Marcin Grabias , Senior Drupal Engineer and maintainer of the Drupal LMS module, used Claude Code to merge two near-duplicate activity plugins into a single configurable one in roughly ten minutes of work that would have taken an hour by hand. The Drupal LMS module lets site builders define quiz-style "activities": question types a learner answers as part of a course. For a while, LMS shipped two separate plugins for selection-based questions: one for single-choice answers, one for multiple-choice.
