Introducing Language Audit and Configuration Language Lock for Drupal and asking for your feedback I am neck deep in Drupal configuration language recently as I was diagnosing problems with how recipes are installed on foreign language Drupal CMS sites and how Drupal Canvas operates under that inconsistent configuration. I did a lot of research and while already fixed a few of the side-quest bugs fund, there are many old standing issues that this area connects. As a result I built two open source Drupal modules: one to diagnose and another to force language behavior.
